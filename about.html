About One V One

Built for players who'd rather prove it on the scoreboard than argue about it in chat.

Who we are

One V One is a competitive gaming platform built around head-to-head duels, squad matchups, and bracket tournaments across today's most popular titles. We handle the matchmaking, the rules, and the payout — you handle the winning.

Fair play, first

Every match is reviewed if it's disputed, every payout is logged, and every player agrees to the same rules before they queue up. We're not trying to be a casino — matches are decided by who plays better, not by chance.
